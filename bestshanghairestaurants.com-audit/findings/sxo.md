# Search Experience Optimization (SXO) Findings

## Page-Type Analysis

### Homepage - Curated Directory Home
Matches user intent well. Clear value proposition above the fold, navigation to key sections. The 3-column restaurant card grid is scannable. FAQ section at bottom adds informational value. **No major page-type mismatch detected.**

### /best-shanghai-restaurants/ - Ranked List
Clean ranked list layout with restaurant cards. Title clearly communicates "15 Best Shanghai Restaurants." Could benefit from a table of contents at top for quick navigation.

### /blog/ - Blog Index
Functions as a standard blog listing. H1 "Blog" is weak (noted in content findings). Could add category filters or featured post treatment.

### Restaurant Detail Pages - Review/Profile
Clean format with name, rating, description, and practical info. Missing user-review signals and "similar restaurants" cross-links.

### Cuisine Pages - Category Hub
Thin content (noted in content findings). These should be richer category pages that drive discovery traffic.

## Persona Scoring

| Persona | Page Fit | Notes |
