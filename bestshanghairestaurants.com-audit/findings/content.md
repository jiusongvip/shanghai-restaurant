# Content Quality Findings
**Score: 78/100 (weight: 23%)**

## What's Working
- Strong E-E-A-T signals: Michelin references, dish expertise, seasonal knowledge
- Substantive long-form content on pillar pages (cuisine guide, xiaolongbao, shengjianbao)
- FAQ section addresses real user queries with natural language answers
- Restaurant detail pages include specific dish recommendations and dining tips
- Consistent brand voice -- authoritative yet approachable food writing

## Findings

### [Medium] Cuisine sub-pages have thin content
Pages like /cuisine/benbang/ have meta descriptions of only ~70 characters and minimal on-page content. These category hubs lack the depth to rank independently.
**Fix**: Expand each cuisine sub-page to 300-500 words with dish examples, restaurant links, and historical context.

### [Medium] Blog index H1 is just "Blog"
'<h1>Blog</h1>' is too generic to match meaningful search queries.
**Fix**: Change H1 to "Shanghai Restaurant Stories, Guides &amp; Food Culture" or similar.

### [Low] No author bylines or publication dates on blog posts
Reduces E-E-A-T signals for Google's content credibility assessment.
**Fix**: Add visible publication dates and author byline.

### [Low] Thin utility pages in sitemap
Privacy policy and reservations pages consume crawl budget without ranking potential.
**Fix**: Add noindex to privacy policy page.
